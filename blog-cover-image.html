Blog Cover Image Generator

Make a header image for a post you already wrote.

A post is finished and the only thing missing is the picture at the top. Stock sites will sell you a photo that four other blogs used this month; this makes one that belongs to your post and nobody else’s.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate — nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Describe the mood and the composition, not the topic. "An article about productivity" gives the model nothing to draw. "A single desk lamp over an open notebook, muted palette, empty space on the left" gives it a picture and leaves room for your headline.

What it will not do

Ask for 16:9 and keep one side of the frame quiet — text that has to sit on a busy area is the usual reason a cover gets thrown out.
