Writing the prompt

Ask for a mark, not a brand. Say the shape ("a folded paper crane reduced to five straight lines"), the treatment ("flat vector, single ink colour") and the framing ("centred, generous margin").

What it will not do

This produces concepts, not deliverables — no vector file, and lettering is unreliable. Take the direction to a designer; do not ship the pixels.
