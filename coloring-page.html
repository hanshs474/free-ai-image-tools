Coloring Page Generator

Line art on white, printable, at whatever difficulty you need.

A rainy afternoon and a printer is the whole use case. The difficulty is adjustable in a way a colouring book is not: thick outlines and few regions for a four-year-old, dense pattern work for an adult.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate — nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Say "line art", "no shading" and "white background" explicitly. Without all three the model reaches for grey fills, and grey fills defeat the point.

What it will not do

Thin, dense lines look impressive on screen and disappear on a home printer. Ask for bold outlines if it is going on paper.

Where this runs

The generator embedded here is the image upscaler and the prompt library from Veida, running on its anonymous free tier.
