Sticker Generator

Flat, bold, and readable at thumbnail size.

Stickers are the easiest thing on this list to get right, because the constraints that make a good sticker are the same constraints that make a model behave: one subject, flat colour, heavy outline.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate — nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Say "die-cut sticker", "thick white border" and "flat colours". Those three phrases do most of the work; everything else is the subject.

What it will not do

Backgrounds come back opaque. For a real cutout you will still need to remove the background afterwards.

Where this runs

The generator embedded here is the background remover and the AI image generator from Veida, running on its anonymous free tier.
