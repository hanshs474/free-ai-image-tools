Product Mockup Generator

Put a product in a scene before the product exists.

Before there is inventory there is usually a landing page, and the landing page needs a photo. This renders the shot you would have booked: one object, one surface, one light source, styled the way a product photographer would style it.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate — nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Name the material and the light. "Matte black ceramic" and "soft window light from the left" are the two decisions that separate a render that reads as a photo from one that reads as clip art.

What it will not do

Logos and packaging copy will not come back legible. Leave the branding off and add it afterwards in any editor.
