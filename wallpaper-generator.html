Wallpaper Generator

A desktop or phone background made for your screen shape.

Wallpaper is the one image most people look at more than any other, and the good ones are almost always the ones with nothing happening in them. Abstract gradients and quiet textures survive having icons dropped on top.

The box above is live. Type over the prompt and press generate — nothing is stored and no account is asked for.

Writing the prompt

Ask for no subject. "Abstract gradient, deep indigo into warm amber, soft grain" is a better wallpaper brief than any scene, because a scene competes with your icons.

What it will not do

Generate at the shape of the screen it is for — 16:9 for a desktop, 9:16 for a phone. Stretching afterwards is visible.

Where this runs

The generator embedded here is the Seedream 5.0 Lite and the free tier limits from Veida, running on its anonymous free tier.

Images on this site are generated by the free tier of Veida — no sign-up and no API key. It is watermarked at 1K; signing in removes the mark and unlocks the larger models.
